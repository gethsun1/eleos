About Eleos

A place for care, connection and hope.

Eleos Wellness & Rehabilitation Centre is based in Gatitu, Thika, Kenya. We welcome conversations from people and families seeking information about wellness and rehabilitation support.

Who we are

Compassion is at the heart of Eleos.

Eleos Wellness & Rehabilitation Centre welcomes individuals and families seeking information about wellness and rehabilitation support in Gatitu, Thika.

Every person arrives with a story that deserves to be heard. People should never be reduced to the challenges they face; they deserve understanding, respect and the opportunity to take their next steps with support.

What guides us

Care shaped by compassion, dignity, connection and hope.

01

Compassion

We begin by listening, meeting each person with patience, empathy and an open mind.

02

Dignity

Every person deserves to be seen beyond their circumstances and treated with respect.

03

Connection

We recognise the strength found in supportive relationships, shared understanding and community.

04

Hope

We believe that change can begin with one conversation, one decision and one supported step forward.

Our mission and vision

A direction for the care we seek to offer.

Mission

To create a welcoming and respectful environment where individuals and families can access thoughtful wellness and rehabilitation support, rediscover their strengths and take meaningful steps towards healthier lives.

Vision

A community where every person can seek support without stigma, experience care with dignity and find the opportunity to grow, recover and flourish.

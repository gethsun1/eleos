Privacy

Privacy Policy

How information shared through Eleos website forms is handled.

Information you provide

If enabled, the enquiry form collects your name, email address, optional telephone number, enquiry category and message. The review form collects a rating, optional comment and an optional display name. Reviews are held for moderation before publication. Please do not submit health histories, diagnoses or other sensitive details.

How information is used

Enquiries are stored in the configured Supabase database so authorised Eleos administrators can respond. Review submissions are stored for moderation. Only approved reviews are shown publicly. The site does not currently send submissions by email.

Storage and access

Submissions are stored with Supabase when the organisation configures the service. Database access is restricted by row-level security policies. Public visitors can submit records and read approved reviews; authorised administrators can access submissions for their stated purposes. Supabase and its edge infrastructure may process network connection details to provide the service.

Abuse prevention

The submission service applies a per-network rate limit. It stores a salted hash of the network address for this purpose rather than saving the address in the application tables. The hosting and edge service may process connection metadata under its own service terms.

Retention and deletion

For questions about a submission or to request its removal, contact Eleos at the email address below.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not intentionally use advertising trackers. The Supabase administrator sign-in may store an authentication session in the browser. Analytics configuration, if added, must be disclosed here.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact eleoswellnesscentre@gmail.com.

Our story

A belief in the person behind the story.

Seeking support is personal. Each person deserves to be approached without judgement, with listening, respect and connection.

A place for the next step

Every person arrives with a story that deserves to be heard.

Some are navigating difficult seasons; others are supporting someone they love. At Eleos, people should never be reduced to the challenges they face. They deserve understanding, respect and the opportunity to take their next steps with support.

E

Care begins by listening, recognising a person’s strengths and making room for what comes next.

Individuals, families and community

Wellbeing grows through understanding and connection.

Wellbeing touches more than one part of life. Supportive relationships, shared understanding and a sense of belonging can all matter as people consider their next steps.

Eleos is based in Gatitu, Thika, and welcomes enquiries from individuals and families looking for information about wellness and rehabilitation support.
